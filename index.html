About SK Solar Energy

Building a cleaner, solar-powered future.

SK Solar Energy is a solar energy business focused on helping customers understand and adopt clean power solutions. We aim to provide clear guidance, suitable system options and dependable customer support.

  • Solutions for homes, businesses and industries
  • On-grid, off-grid and hybrid system guidance
  • Project consultation and system design
  • Service enquiries across Bihar and Rajasthan

We first understand your electricity use, site conditions and budget, then help identify a suitable solar solution. Final system specifications, pricing, approvals and timelines are confirmed after a site assessment.
